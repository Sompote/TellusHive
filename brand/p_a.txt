A macOS app icon for TellusHive, a desktop program where a team of AI agents does civil and geotechnical engineering design. Square 1024x1024 canvas, one rounded-square tile (macOS Big Sur style, about 824 px wide, centred, transparent outside the tile, soft subtle shadow). Flat modern vector style with gentle depth, crisp edges, no text, no letters, no words, no watermark. It must read clearly at 32 px, so use few bold shapes.

Concept A, "the hive in the ground": deep navy-to-blue gradient tile. In the centre, a honeycomb of seven hexagonal cells. The lower cells are filled with horizontal soil strata in warm earth tones (ochre sand, brown clay, grey rock band) like a borehole section, so the hive grows out of the ground. The upper cells are light and hold small white nodes joined by thin lines, a network of agents. One amber glowing node at the centre stands for the coordinator.
